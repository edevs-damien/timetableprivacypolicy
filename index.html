This Privacy Policy applies to the TimeTable ISA application (hereby referred to as "the App") for mobile devices, developed by Damien Schenker. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how the App functions.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

The App operates entirely locally on your device. We do not use any analytics, user tracking, crash reporting services, or advertising frameworks that monitor your behavior or collect Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

2. Third-Party Services and Local Data

To provide its core functionality, the App downloads your personal educational schedule directly from isa.fr.ch. Please note the following important details regarding this process:

  • Direct Connection: The App establishes a direct connection from your device to isa.fr.ch. Your login credentials, session tokens, and downloaded schedules never pass through servers owned or operated by us.
  • Strictly Local Storage: Once the schedule data is successfully downloaded, it is stored strictly within the secure local storage of your device to enable offline access. We have no access to this locally stored data.
  • Third-Party Policies: Since the App connects directly to isa.fr.ch, your network interactions with that service are subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service. We do not own or control the IS-Academia platform.

3. Security

Because all application data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us, the security of your schedule data relies on the physical and software security measures of your own device (e.g., screen locks, biometrics, and device encryption). We recommend keeping your device updated and secured.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately upon posting.
